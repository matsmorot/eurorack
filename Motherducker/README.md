# The Motherducker
The Motherducker is a sidechain compressor first made by L.Geerinckx as a perfboard version. I just made my own PCB and panel, plus added a bi-color LED to visualise the negative parts.
You connect your kick and signal you want to duck, and tweak the knobs to get your desired results. No need for separate envelope generator and VCA. Read more about it on its original GitHub home. Link below.
